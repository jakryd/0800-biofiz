1 (3p). Describe a simple method for estimating the size of a water molecule 
based on the concepts of the mole, Avogadro's number, and the density of water. 
Perform the estimation.

2 (3p). Fumarase (fumarate hydratase) is an enzyme that catalyzes the addition 
of a water molecule to fumarate, producing malate:

Fumarate + $H_2O$ = Malate

Calculate the standard Gibbs free energy change, ΔG° of this reaction, given 
the following standard Gibbs free energies of formation:

Fumarate:    $\Delta G^0 = -604.62$ kJ/mol    
Malate:      $\Delta G^0 = - 845.65$ kJ/mol   
$H_2O$:         $\Delta G^0 = - 273.35$ kJ/mol   

3 (3p). State the Nernst–Planck electrodiffusion equation, and then use a graph 
created in Excel to estimate how the membrane potential depends on the ratio of 
ion concentrations (e.g., $Na^+$) outside and inside the cell membrane.

4 (3p). Introduce and describe the basic form of the Verlet algorithm 
(without velocity dependence). Use a Taylor series expansion.

5 (3p). List the basic molecular dynamics methods suitable for simulating a 
system containing a single atom. Briefly describe each of them.
